# Visual credits

The four images in `screenshots/` are on-device screenshots captured by Max Seelig (MAXmade) on the original Canon EOS M on 2026-10-21. They show the Mirror Worlds controls and LiveView, with camera overlays intact; they are not exported CR2 photos. The original `VRAM0.BMP`, `VRAM4.BMP`, `VRAM5.BMP` and `VRAM6.BMP` were converted to lossless WebP at their original 720 × 480 resolution.

The seaside scene is an AI-generated photographic illustration created with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool on 2026-10-21. It is not a photograph captured with a Canon EOS M or evidence of camera output. The illustrative mirror demonstrations are computed in the browser; no AI is used by the Magic Lantern module.

Prompt: "An asymmetrical, sunlit concrete seaside promenade with terracotta stairs, weathered off-white brutalist arches, a palm, teal sea and warm afternoon shadows. Tactile travel-photography feeling, no lettering, no mirroring or symmetry. Landscape 3:2."
