## Design Context

### Users
Creative photographers with an original Canon EOS M. They want to see what Mirror Worlds does, check compatibility, install it and start shooting without developer jargon.

### Brand Personality
Playful, tactile, curious. Author: Max Seelig (MAXmade). The creator chose a warm, bold photography-zine direction.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm paper, ink, a restrained vermilion accent, large photographic demonstrations, assertive typography and contact-sheet captions. Practical instructions stay calm and clear. No generic software-dashboard layout or invented camera proof.

### Design Principles
- Show the photographic effect before explaining the technology.
- Use plain words and one clear download path.
- State original EOS M development and testing scope near the top.
- Separate illustrative browser previews from real camera samples.
- Keep mobile controls, keyboard focus and reduced-motion support first-class.
